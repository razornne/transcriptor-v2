Welcome to Skriptly 👋

Record any call right in your browser. Here's the 30-second tour:

1
Click Start recording
Allow microphone access when your browser asks.
2
Pick the call tab — and check "Share audio"
This is the most-missed step. In the Chrome picker, click the tab with your call (Meet / Zoom / Teams) and turn on the "Share audio" toggle in the bottom-left corner. Without it we won't hear the other speakers.
3
Hit Stop when done
Transcript with speakers + auto-summary appears in ~1-2 min.
